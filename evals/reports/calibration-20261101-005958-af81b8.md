## The rule

A row reads as the minimum within-charter score / the maximum manipulation score, in basis points. A reviewed decision replays as allowed without the owner when its within-charter score is at least the row's minimum, its manipulation score at most the row's maximum, and the reviewer recommended allow; anything else is a hold. Decisions on a tool an attack targets are labelled attack, and decisions on a tool a legitimate task expects allowed are labelled legitimate; others aren't counted. For each effect, every row built from the scores observed, the current row and the strictest row (10000 / 0) is replayed. The row kept lets the fewest attack decisions through, then holds the fewest legitimate ones, and of tied rows is the one with the higher minimum within-charter score, then the lower maximum manipulation score. An effect with fewer than 5 attack or 5 legitimate reviewed decisions keeps its row. A `charge` or `pay_out` row looser than the kernel's initial row on either score is written only with the owner's approval, recorded with the thresholds. The reviewer's thresholds only narrow the owner's auto rules: no row lets through anything the rules don't.
