# Spanish words free prose may not use: amounts, percentages, dates, promises and bookings reach a
# visitor only in cards rendered from the business's records. One `<category>\t<regex>` per line,
# matched case-insensitively as whole words on the prose with accents and other marks dropped
# ("dólares" is matched as "dolares"), each run of whitespace read as one space and every dash and
# hyphen as "-" ("fin‐de‐semana" with U+2010 is "fin-de-semana"). Categories: number, money,
# percent and date (number_in_prose), and promise, booking and callback (promise_word). A booking
# or call claim with a condition straight before it ("cuando tu cita está confirmada") passes (`BOOKING_CONDITION`
# in reply_check.py), but not after "sí" (yes), whose accent tells it from "si" (if); every
# booking claim passes in a reply that carries the booking's card. Call claims instead need a
# callback_confirmed card that passed the clause checks; neither card exempts the other category.
# Both lexicons run on every draft, whatever its language.

# Spelled-out numbers. A bare "uno", "una", "once" or "medio" counts only next to a unit or another
# number word, so "una pregunta" passes; "las once" and "la una" are clock times (below).

number	dieci(?:seis|siete|ocho|nueve)|veinte|veinti[a-z]+
number	treinta|cuarenta|cincuenta|sesenta|setenta|ochenta|noventa
number	cien|cientos?|(?:dos|tres|cuatro|seis|sete|ocho|nove)cient[oa]s|quinient[oa]s
number	mil|miles|millon(?:es)?|docenas?|mitad
number	ceros?
# "cuarto" is a fraction, a time ("y cuarto"), an ordinal and a room, and is blocked whichever
# it is: a reply naming a room needs another word for it ("habitación").
number	cuart[oa]s?
number	tercios?|terceras?[\s-]+partes?
# Fractions from a fifth to a tenth ("un quinto", "una décima parte"); "séptimo" and
# "décimo" without the accent. "décimos" is left out: without its accent it is "decimos" (we
# tell), and as a fraction it always follows a number word ("dos décimos"), blocked above.
number	quint[oa]s?|sext[oa]s?|sep?tim[oa]s?|octav[oa]s?|noven[oa]s?|decim(?:o|a|as)
number	(?:uno|una|once|medio)[\s-]+(?:y[\s-]+)?(?:cien|cientos?|mil|miles|millon(?:es)?|docenas?|dolar(?:es)?|pesos?|euros?|centavos?|por[\s-]?ciento)

# Money ("dólar" and "dólares" are matched without the accent)
money	dolar(?:es)?|pesos?|euros?|centavos?|usd|mxn

# Percentages
percent	por[\s-]?ciento|porcentajes?

# Days, months and times ("miércoles", "sábado", "mañana", "mediodía", "día" without accents),

date	las[\s-]+once|la[\s-]+una

# A time soon: "en" or "dentro de" and up to three words before minutes or hours ("en una
# hora", "dentro de un par de horas", "en media hora"). The bare adverb "enseguida" ("en
# seguida") is no time word (the owner's decision of 2026-10-30), so urgent advice passes
# ("Cierre la llave de paso enseguida.").
date	(?:en|dentro[\s-]+de)[\s-]+(?:[^\W\d_]+[\s-]+){0,3}(?:minutos?|mins?|horas?|hrs?)
date	(?:a[\s-]+)?primera[\s-]+hora

# Promises only the owner's rules or a card can make ("garantía" without the accent). "libre"
# states a price before "para", "a" or "al" and everyone, an audience or "usted", "ti" or "vos":
# "acceso libre a todas las clases", "libre para los socios", "la entrada es libre al público",
# "libre para usted", "libre para vosotros". It passes elsewhere, where it is a time that is open
# or a freedom: "un horario libre", "tengo la tarde libre", "libre para la cita", "usted es libre
# de preguntar". "entrada libre" and "libre de costo", "de cargo", "de pago" or "de impuestos" are
# a price wherever they stand, and so are "sin costo", "sin coste", "sin cargo" and "sin recargo"
# (no surcharge), in the plural too, whatever word follows ("sin cargo extra", "sin costos
# adicionales"). So is "no hay", "no tiene" or "no lleva" (or "no tienen", "no llevan") with any
# one or two words before a charge, a cost or a surcharge: "No hay cargo extra.", "No tiene
# costo.", "no lleva ningún recargo", "no hay cargos ocultos".
promise	garantias?|garantiz[a-z]*|promet[a-z]*|promesas?
promise	gratis|gratuit[oa]s?|descuentos?|descont[a-z]*|reembols[a-z]*
promise	sin[\s-]+(?:ningun[\s-]+)?(?:costos?|costes?|cargos?)|de[\s-]+regalo
promise	sin[\s-]+recargos?
promise	no[\s-]+(?:hay|tienen?|llevan?)[\s-]+(?:[^\W\d_]+[\s-]+){0,2}(?:cargos?|costos?|costes?|recargos?)
promise	sin[\s-]+(?:ninguna[\s-]+)?comision(?:es)?
promise	por[\s-]+nuestra[\s-]+cuenta|(?:por[\s-]+)?cuenta[\s-]+de[\s-]+la[\s-]+casa
promise	entradas?[\s-]+libres?
promise	libres?[\s-]+de[\s-]+(?:costos?|costes?|cargos?|pagos?|impuestos?)
promise	libres?(?=[\s-]+(?:para|a)[\s-]+(?:tod[oa]s|todo[\s-]+el[\s-]+mundo|cualquiera)\b)
promise	libres?(?=[\s-]+(?:(?:para|a)[\s-]+(?:(?:el|la|l[oa]s|nuestr[oa]s?|sus?|tus?)[\s-]+)?|al[\s-]+)(?:soci[oa]s?|miembros?|clientes?|publico|usted(?:es)?|ti|vos(?:otros|otras)?)\b)

# Booking confirmations, which only a booking card makes, written from a claim's parts rather than
# sample phrasings ("está", "estás", "están" and "quedó" without the accent). A claim is a booking
# noun or none, a verb (está, estás, queda, quedó, fue, ha
# quedado, ha sido, in the singular and the plural), and a participle (confirmado, reservado,
# agendado, apartado, hecho, in either gender and number): "tu cita está confirmada", "su turno
# quedó reservado", "las citas están confirmadas", "su reserva quedó hecha", "ya estás
# reservado". The first line takes the determiner and one more word before the noun, so a
# condition before the whole claim is seen ("cuando su próxima cita está confirmada"). The second
# holds the verb and the participle whatever comes before it ("la cita de Ana está confirmada",
# "la suya está confirmada", "listo, quedó apartado") and starts at a whole-word "ya" when it is
# there, but with "hecho" only before a booking noun ("quedó hecha la reserva"), since otherwise it
# says what something is made of ("el tubo está hecho de cobre"). A claim inside a longer one is
# that claim, so one claim is one violation. Every noun position uses the same alternation,
# covering the appointments different businesses book, in both numbers, including multiword
# names ("visita guiada", "corte de pelo"). It keeps the whole claim after its condition
# ("cuando tu mesa está confirmada"). "Guardado" and "listo" need a booking noun, so saving
# details or finishing another task makes no booking claim ("tus datos están guardados").
booking	(?:tus?|sus?|el|la|los|las|una?|mis?|est[ea]s?|es[ea]s?|nuestr[oa]s?)[\s-]+(?:[^\W\d_]+[\s-]+)?(?:citas?|reservas?|visitas?[\s-]+guiadas?|visitas?|turnos?|horas?|plazas?|recorridos?|clases?|leccion(?:es)?|sesion(?:es)?|mesas?|habitacion(?:es)?|estancias?|estadias?|alojamientos?|consultas?|pruebas?|degustacion(?:es)?|tratamientos?|masajes?|cortes?[\s-]+de[\s-]+pelo|demostracion(?:es)?|recogidas?|reunion(?:es)?)[\s-]+(?:ya[\s-]+)?(?:estan?|quedan?|quedo|quedaron|fu(?:e|eron)|ha[ns]?[\s-]+(?:quedado|sido))[\s-]+(?:confirmad|reservad|agendad|apartad|programad|hech|guardad|list)[oa]s?
