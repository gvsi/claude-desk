# English words free prose may not use: amounts, percentages, dates, promises and bookings reach a
# visitor only in cards rendered from the business's records. One `<category>\t<regex>` per line,
# matched case-insensitively as whole words on the prose with accents and other marks dropped, each
# run of whitespace read as one space, every dash and hyphen as "-" ("next‐week" with U+2010 is
# "next-week") and every apostrophe as "’" ("oʼclock" is "o’clock"). Categories: number, money,
# percent and date (number_in_prose), and promise, booking and callback (promise_word). A booking
# or call claim with a condition straight before it ("once you're booked") passes (`BOOKING_CONDITION` in
# reply_check.py), and so does every booking claim in a reply that carries the booking's card.
# Call claims instead need a callback_confirmed card that passed the clause checks; neither
# card exempts the other category. Both lexicons run on every draft, whatever its language.
