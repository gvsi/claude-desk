# Reference boards

The design system is checked against the design canvas boards the project owner signed off, listed once each in `REFERENCE_BOARDS` (`boards.ts`). Run 2's boards (`N-Run2-*`, `N-R2-*`), drawn on 2026-10-29, are listed too while they are in review: each one's story says "built against the board in review; sign-off pending" until the owner signs it. Every story names its board in `parameters.canvas`, or says `'none'` with a `canvasNote` naming the nearest board, and the project's story hooks fail a story that doesn't.

## How a board is checked

- **A board story.** Each board is rebuilt by one `Boards/*` story in `web/src/ds/boards/`, from the components and the fixtures. What no component draws, because the web app (plan 04) owns that page's layout, is composed beside the stories in `boards/parts.tsx` and `boards/boards.css`, on the tokens. Each story's `canvasNote` lists what differs from its board.
- **Its play.** It asserts the board spec below with `expectComputed`, checks that receipts sit 8 px under their message on its side, and walks the page with Tab (`focus_order_is_logical`). The spec rows are `boards/spec.ts`; a row that differs between boards is one row per board.
- **By eye, and by the pixel.** Each story is compared with its board's render at the window the board is drawn at, where the values the spec asserts are also read off the render (colors, edges and spacing, pixel for pixel), then checked at 1440×900 and 390×844 for sideways scroll and clipped text, with a keyboard walk that shows the focus edge at every stop (1 px of `line-focus` inside a borderless control, a bordered one's own border in `line-focus`, a text link's words and 2 px underline in `text-primary`) but a text field's, which shows its caret and a stronger edge. A value the render shows is the one the story matches; only content, and the decisions signed after a board, may differ from it.
- **Each board at its own window.** Most boards are drawn at 1280×820, the Storybook "Canvas" viewport. The recorded-history boards, the Connectors tab and Rules boards (`N-Conn-*`) and run 2's laptop boards are drawn at a 14-inch laptop's 1512×982, the "14-inch laptop" viewport; N-Conn-24-Phone-Connectors and run 2's phone boards at a phone's 390×844, the "Phone" viewport; and a run 2 page longer than the window at the laptop's width and its own height (1512×994, 1512×1061, 1512×1100, 1512×1150 or 1512×1500), a viewport of its own. The vision boards (`N-R2-Vision-*`) are laptop boards under the vision bar, the team Desk's story a 1061 px page and the Claude account's a 994 px one, as their components measure them, while their canvas boards are 1022 px and 1013 px tall; the city office's resident page (N-R2-Vision-CityOffice-Resident) runs past the window, so its render is captured at its full height, 1512×1491, as the `N-Conn-*` boards are, and its story checks the page's own height. N-Docs-Sidebar is the sidebar alone, 264×982, the "Sidebar alone" viewport. `BOARD_VIEWPORTS` and `boardViewport` in `boards.ts` say which, the preview builds its viewports from them, and each board story sets its board's viewport in `globals`, so its play runs at that window in Vitest too. The `N-Conn-*` boards and N-R2-Vision-CityOffice-Resident are captured at their full height (`capturedAtFullHeight`), and a tall one is compared at its render's height.
- **The mark.** Boards drawn before 2026-10-30 show the earlier counter mark; the hand-drawn bell (N-Explore-Mark-Bell, signed 2026-10-30) replaced it everywhere, so a story draws the bell where its board draws the counter. The bell has no tile (N-Explore-Mark-Decided, signed 2026-10-30): its ink is light on the dark grounds and dark on the intro illustration's light card. The About pages' top bar shows the app's lockup, and the white tile is the app icon above the article's title (N-Docs-Hero-Proposal, option A, signed the same day). Third-party connector marks have no tile either (N-Explore-Connector-Decided, signed the same day), so a story draws them bare where its board draws a white tile. The sidebar's one-line lockup, the 36 px bell, "Claude Desk" and the "Preview" badge (N-Explore-Mark-Lockup-Inline, signed 2026-10-30), was signed after the boards that draw the sidebar, so their sidebar lockup, a 20 px mark beside "Claude Desk" with "Beta" under it, differs from the story's.
- **Which window each check uses.** A board story's play runs at its board's window (`boardViewport`, from `BOARD_WINDOWS`). A render is checked at its board's size (`renderSize`): the same window, except for a board whose page the components measure at another height than the canvas drew it, which keeps the canvas's size in `RENDER_SIZES`. The team Desk's story runs at 1512×1061 and its render is 1512×1022; the Claude account's story at 1512×994 and its render 1512×1013. `render_size_apart_from_story_window` holds both.
- **The renders stay out of the repository.** They are rendered from the canvas to a folder on the checking machine. `boards.test.ts` checks there is one per board, at its board's render size (`renderSize`) (1280×820, 1512×982 for the laptop boards, 264×982 for the sidebar alone, 390×844 for a phone, or a run 2 page's own height; an `N-Conn-*` board or N-R2-Vision-CityOffice-Resident its window's width and at least its height), when `DS_REFERENCE_DIR` names that folder, and skips otherwise. It also checks that every board story runs at its board's window:

```sh
cd web
DS_REFERENCE_DIR=/path/to/reference npx vitest run --project unit src/ds/reference
npx vitest run --project storybook src/ds/boards
```
