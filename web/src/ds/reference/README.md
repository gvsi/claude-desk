# Reference boards

The design system is checked against the design canvas boards the project owner signed off, listed once each in `REFERENCE_BOARDS` (`boards.ts`). Every story names its board in `parameters.canvas`, or says `'none'` with a `canvasNote` naming the nearest board, and the project's story hooks fail a story that doesn't.

## How a board is checked

- **A board story.** Each board is rebuilt by one `Boards/*` story in `web/src/ds/boards/`, from the components and the fixtures. What no component draws, because the web app (plan 04) owns that page's layout, is composed beside the stories in `boards/parts.tsx` and `boards/boards.css`, on the tokens. Each story's `canvasNote` lists what differs from its board.
- **Its play.** It asserts the board spec below with `expectComputed`, checks that receipts sit 8 px under their message on its side, and walks the page with Tab (`focus_order_is_logical`). The spec rows are `boards/spec.ts`; a row that differs between boards is one row per board.
- **By eye, and by the pixel.** Each story is compared with its board's render at the size the board is drawn at, where the values the spec asserts are also read off the render (colors, edges and spacing, pixel for pixel), then checked at 1440×900 and 390×844 for sideways scroll and clipped text, with a keyboard walk that shows the 2 px blue ring at every stop. A value the render shows is the one the story matches; only content, and the decisions signed after a board, may differ from it.
- **Render sizes.** The owner, public and first-visit boards are rendered at 1280×820. The Connectors and rules boards (`N-Conn-*`) are drawn at a 14-inch laptop's width, so they are rendered 1512 px wide (N-Conn-16-Phone-Rules 390 px wide) and captured at full height; their stories run at the Storybook "Board" viewport, 1512×982 (the phone board's at "Phone", 390×844), and a tall board is compared at its render's height.
- **The renders stay out of the repository.** They are rendered from the canvas to a folder on the checking machine. `boards.test.ts` checks there is one per board, at its size (1280×820; or 1512 px wide and at least 982 tall, 390 wide and at least 844 tall for the phone board), when `DS_REFERENCE_DIR` names that folder, and skips otherwise:

```sh
cd web
DS_REFERENCE_DIR=/path/to/reference npx vitest run --project unit src/ds/reference
npx vitest run --project storybook src/ds/boards
```
